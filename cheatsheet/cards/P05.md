# Paper 5: Fløysand & Jakobsen (2016)
<!-- card: P05 | cite: Fløysand & Jakobsen 2016 | pages: 323-346 | class: 2 | themes: TH2 -->
*Business development in Western Norway: adaptability and regional renewal (in Norwegian)*, Book chapter in Region og regionalisering, Novus Forlag.

## In one line
Western Norway renews through related variety and entrepreneurship, while mature clusters mostly extend what they do.

## Key concepts
- **Regional innovation system** (*regionalt innovasjonssystem*): citing Cooke and colleagues, interacting subsystems of knowledge infrastructure and commercial exploitation. (p. 326)
- **Related variety** (*relatert variasjon*): industries that differ but are related, which gives a region room to combine them. (p. 330)
- **Path extension** (*baneforlengelse*): more of the same, continuing an existing industrial path. (p. 328)
- **Path creation** (*baneskaping*): developing a new industry. (p. 328)

## Model
**Three levels of regional renewal** (p. 324):
1. Industry level: cluster dynamics.
2. Firm level: related variety.
3. Individual level: entrepreneurship.

## Findings
- Specialisation can breed continuity and stagnation, so related industries and international networks matter. (p. 324)
- Ten of eighteen western economic areas score above the national median for related variety. (p. 337)
- The region's share of national secondary employment rose from 24 to 33 percent between 1970 and 2010. (pp. 331–332)

## Norway link
- **Mongstad microalgae**: a pilot that would use carbon dioxide from oil and gas to grow fish feed. (p. 337)
- **The cluster programme**: Western Norway's clusters are recognised at Arena, NCE and GCE levels. (p. 333)

## Exam use
- Jun 2025 Q2: related variety can promote restructuring, while specialisation can inhibit it. (our reading)

## Limits
- The Ulstein interview came from a cluster study and was not designed for this question. (p. 340)
- Which driver matters most cannot be settled. (p. 343)

## Hook
Four counties, three levels, one path bending.
