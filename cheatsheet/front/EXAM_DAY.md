## Must-know definitions
1. Innovation system: learning and innovation across the whole economy, not only universities. (Fagerberg et al. 2009, p. 431)
2. National innovation system: the country-level version, from Freeman, Lundvall and Nelson. (Fagerberg et al. 2009, p. 431)
3. Regional innovation system: citing Cooke, interacting subsystems of knowledge and commercial exploitation. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 16)
4. Technological innovation system: citing Carlsson, the agents around one technology. (Hekkert et al. 2007, p. 416)
5. Entrepreneurial ecosystem: citing Hawley, co-evolution, mutualistic interdependence and diverse, nested actors. (Stam & van de Ven 2021, p. 811)
6. Institutions: citing North, the formal and informal rules that shape action. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 16)
7. Institutional work: citing Lawrence, work that actors do on institutions. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 15)
8. Agency: following Bækkelund, change agency alters a path and reproductive agency keeps it going. (Bækkelund 2021, p. 759)
9. Path dependence: history that favours some solutions and holds others back. (Fagerberg et al. 2009, p. 431)
10. Path creation: citing MacKinnon, new development trajectories emerging in a region. (Njøs et al. 2020, p. 268)
11. Sustainable transformation: citing UNEP, change that cuts emissions and protects biodiversity. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 17)
12. Sustainability: ecological continuity, justice within and between generations, quality of life. (Schlaile et al. 2017, p. 5)

## How to answer
- Question 1: start with a direct definition (By X, I understand …) and show how the readings and classes use the term. [Class 9, slides 34–35]
- Question 2 or 3: introduction [Class 9, slide 39], main part [Class 9, slide 40], conclusion [Class 9, slide 41].
- What examiners look for [Class 9, slide 43]:
- [ ] Structure: introduction, theory, empirical material and conclusion, each with its weight.
- [ ] Problem statement: follow the question you were given, and show how you read it.
- [ ] Language and argument: independence, an argument carried through, a tidy presentation.
- [ ] Theory: the readings put to use, relevant and understood, fitted to the problem.
- [ ] Analysis: relevant cases, analysed critically and related to theory.
- [ ] Conclusion: back to the problem, the findings summed up, the limits stated.

## Promote or inhibit
- Promotes: related variety lets a region recombine what it has into a new path. (Fløysand & Jakobsen 2016, p. 330)
- Inhibits: a specialized, dirigiste system can keep a region in one industry. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 15)
- Promotes: a cluster and public procurement can create a market for a new technology. (Njøs et al. 2020, pp. 281–282)
- Inhibits: weak market formation and weak materialization hold a technology back. (Steen et al. 2024, p. 9)
- Promotes: collective institutional work can legitimize a green shift. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 15)
- Inhibits: institutional work can unintentionally sustain oil and gas. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 25)
