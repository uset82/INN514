*CCS technological innovation system dynamics in Norway*, International Journal of Greenhouse Gas Control 136.

## In one line
The Norwegian CCS system has grown fast, but its market, resources and material base stay weak.

## Key concepts
- **Technological innovation system** (*teknologisk innovasjonssystem*): actors, networks and institutions that build and spread a technology. (p. 2)
- **Function** (*funksjon*): a process that builds and spreads the technology, eight of them here. (p. 2)
- **Materialization** (*materialisering*): the function for large physical infrastructure, added because CCS needs it. (p. 2)
- **Legitimation** (*legitimering*): the function for acceptance and legitimacy. (p. 3)
- **Market formation** (*markedsforming*): the function that builds a market, rated weak here. (p. 3)

## Model
**Assessing a technological innovation system** (p. 3):
1. Map the actors, networks and institutions.
2. Rate every function as strong, intermediate or weak.
3. Aim policy at the weak functions.

## Findings
- Knowledge is strong, but diffusion is limited because projects are customised and firms protect their ideas. (p. 9)
- Market formation, resource mobilization and materialization are weak. (p. 9)
- Legitimacy rises as CCS moves from fossil energy into waste and process industry. (p. 9)

## Norway link
- **Longship and Northern Lights**: the main exceptions to weak materialization, as large projects being built. (p. 10)
- **Mongstad**: an earlier plan for a full-scale plant at the refinery and power station. (p. 4)

## Exam use
- Jun 2025 Q3: find the weak functions and aim policy at them. (our reading)

## Limits
- Other interviewees might have produced slightly different results. (p. 11)
- CCS innovation is international, so a wider geographical lens would help. (p. 11)

## Hook
Eight functions, one weak chain, a policy target.
