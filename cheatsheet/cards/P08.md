# Paper 8: Autio et al. (2014)
<!-- card: P08 | cite: Autio et al. 2014 | pages: 1097-1108 | class: 4 | themes: TH4 -->
*Entrepreneurial innovation: the importance of context*, Research Policy 43(7).

## In one line
The editors ask when and where new ventures innovate, and answer with six contexts around the entrepreneur, in time and space.

## Key concepts
- **Entrepreneurial innovation** (*entreprenøriell innovasjon*): innovation carried out by new ventures, whose rate varies enormously between countries and regions. (p. 1099)
- **Selection effects** (*seleksjonseffekter*): opportunity costs and social legitimacy decide who becomes an entrepreneur. (p. 1100)
- **Strategic choice effects** (*strategiske valgeffekter*): what those who enter then judge desirable and feasible. (p. 1100)
- **Context** (*kontekst*): the industry, organisation, institutions and social setting around the entrepreneur, in time and space. (p. 1100)
- **Innovation system** (*innovasjonssystem*): the literature that explains structures and institutions but studies agency less. (p. 1098)

## Model
**Six contexts of entrepreneurial innovation** (p. 1100, Fig. 1):
1. Industry and technology.
2. Organisational context.
3. Institutions and policy.
4. Social context.
5. Temporal context.
6. Spatial context.

## Findings
- Fewer than 30 percent of new ventures report products that are new to their customers, so most are not innovative. (p. 1098)
- Innovation systems research explains structures but sidelines agency and entrepreneurship. (p. 1098)
- The interplay of contexts makes different ecosystems produce different kinds of innovation. (p. 1100)
