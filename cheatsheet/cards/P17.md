# Paper 17: Njøs et al. (2020)
<!-- card: P17 | cite: Njøs et al. 2020 | pages: 266-288 | class: 9 | themes: TH5, TH6 -->
*Expanding analyses of path creation: interconnections between territory and technology*, Economic Geography 96(3).

## In one line
Two green technologies in the same region sit in different phases, because territory and technology connect differently.

## Key concepts
- **Path creation** (*baneskaping*): citing MacKinnon and colleagues, the emergence of new development trajectories in a region. (p. 268)
- **Territorial capabilities** (*territorielle kapasiteter*): knowledge sharing, support structures and industrial policy. (p. 266)
- **Guidance of the search, market formation and legitimation** (*retning for søket, markedsforming og legitimering*): the three functions the paper adds to evolutionary economic geography. (p. 271)
- **Preformation** (*preformasjon*): the early phase before a path forms, where the CCS case stays. (p. 281)

## Model
**Three phases of a path**, citing Martin (p. 268):
1. Preformation: before a path forms.
2. Path creation.
3. Path development.

## Findings
- Batteries reached green path creation through regional competence, a cluster and public procurement, reaching some seventy ferry lines. (pp. 281–282)
- CCS stays in preformation: few dedicated firms, no market and top-down policy. (p. 281)
- Territory and technology have to be analysed together. (p. 266)

## Norway link
- **Maritime batteries**: NCE Maritime CleanTech set a direction and public procurement created the market. (pp. 281–282)
- **Technology Centre Mongstad**: the region hosts it, yet CCS stays in preformation. (p. 281)

## Exam use
- Jun 2025 Q2: batteries were promoted by procurement, a cluster and an agency; CCS was held back. (our reading)

## Limits
- The authors leave three questions open, including whether green path creation differs from other industries. (p. 284)

## Hook
Two green technologies, one region, two phases.
