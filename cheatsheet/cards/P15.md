# Paper 15: Garud & Karnøe (2003)
<!-- card: P15 | cite: Garud & Karnøe 2003 | pages: 277-300 | class: 5 | themes: TH5 -->
*Bricolage versus breakthrough: distributed and embedded agency in technology entrepreneurship*, Research Policy 32.

## In one line
Two wind-power paths show how distributed, embedded agency let modest Danish actors beat far better-funded American ones.

## Key concepts
- **Distributed agency** (*distribuert aktørskap*): the inputs that shape a technology come from actors in different domains, each with its own frame. (p. 280)
- **Embedded agency** (*innebygd aktørskap*): the accumulating path begins to enable and constrain the actors who built it. (p. 281)
- **Bricolage** (*bricolage*): citing Levi-Strauss, resourcefulness and improvisation with modest, steady gains. (p. 278)
- **Breakthrough** (*gjennombrudd*): an attempt at dramatic outcomes. (p. 279)
- **Technology entrepreneurship** (*teknologisk entreprenørskap*): the process by which a technology and the actors around it shape each other. (p. 277)

## Model
**The artifact and its embedding processes** (p. 279, Figure 2):
1. Regulation: the rules around the technology.
2. Evaluation: how performance is judged and compared.
3. Design and production: how the artifact is made.
4. Use: how users take it up.

## Findings
- Danish actors with modest resources became world leaders in wind power, against better-funded American actors. (p. 277)
- Agency is distributed across actors with different interpretive frames. (p. 280)
- The path, once built, enables and constrains the actors who built it. (p. 281)
