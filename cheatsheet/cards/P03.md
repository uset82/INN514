# Paper 3: Fløysand et al. (2022)

*Institutional work, regional key actors, and green industrial restructuring*, Norsk Geografisk Tidsskrift – Norwegian Journal of Geography 76(1).

## In one line
Two oil regions share a green vision, but institutional work legitimizes it in Hordaland and delegitimizes it in Rogaland.

## Key concepts
- **Institutional work** (*institusjonelt arbeid*): citing Lawrence and colleagues, the physical and mental work actors do to affect institutions. (p. 15)
- **Regional innovation system** (*regionalt innovasjonssystem*): citing Cooke, interacting subsystems of knowledge generation and exploitation. (p. 16)
- **Institutions** (*institusjoner*): citing North, formal and informal rules that shape action. (p. 16)
- **Institutional logics** (*institusjonelle logikker*): citing Thornton and Almandoz, practice patterns and social prescriptions that legitimize practice. (p. 18)
- **Change and reproductive agency** (*endringsaktørskap og reproduktivt aktørskap*): following Bækkelund, work that alters or keeps a path. (p. 17)

## Model
**Four forms of institutional work**, citing Lawrence and colleagues (p. 17):
1. Maintaining institutions: keeping rules and practices in place.
2. Creating institutions: building new rules and organisations.
3. Changing institutions: reshaping existing rules.
4. Disrupting institutions: breaking them down.

## Findings
- In diversified Hordaland, collective-oriented work legitimizes a green shift. (p. 15)
- In petroleum-specialized Rogaland, individual-oriented work delegitimizes it. (p. 15)
- Institutional work can also sustain oil and gas, against intention. (p. 25)

## Norway link
- **Hordaland and Rogaland**: two adjacent petroleum regions with opposite outcomes. (p. 15)
- **Hordaland crossover**: an intermediary says forty to fifty oil firms now also supply ocean farming. (p. 23)

## Exam use
- Jun 2025 Q1: institutional work and a regional system, through the two cases. (our reading)
- Jun 2025 Q2: Hordaland promotes restructuring, Rogaland inhibits it. (our reading)

## Limits
- The regions differ in history; maritime and salmon farming are stronger in Hordaland. (p. 25)
- More dialogue with interviewees might have raised validity. (p. 19)

## Hook
Same vision, two regions, opposite outcomes.
