## Core definitions
- **Regional innovation system** (*regionalt innovasjonssystem*): citing Cooke, interacting subsystems of knowledge infrastructure and commercial exploitation, set in knowledge, industry and governance. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 16)
- **Related variety** (*relatert variasjon*): industries that differ but are related, which gives a region room to combine them and innovate across industries. (Fløysand & Jakobsen 2016, p. 330)
- **Path extension** (*baneforlengelse*): more of the same, continuing an existing industrial path rather than opening a new one. (Fløysand & Jakobsen 2016, p. 328)
- **Path creation** (*baneskaping*): developing a new industry rather than extending an old one. (Fløysand & Jakobsen 2016, p. 328)
- **Institutional work** (*institusjonelt arbeid*): citing Lawrence and colleagues, the work actors do to affect institutions, whether maintaining, creating, changing or disrupting them. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 15)

## Key models and figures
**Three levels of regional renewal** (Fløysand & Jakobsen 2016, p. 324):
1. Industry level: cluster dynamics, how firms and support institutions interact.
2. Firm level: related variety across industries.
3. Individual level: entrepreneurship, the actors who start something new.

**Four industrial trajectories** (Fløysand & Jakobsen 2016, p. 328):
1. Path exhaustion: an industry weakens and loses its base.
2. Path extension: more of the same.
3. Path renewal: upgrading the industry with new products.
4. Path creation: a new industry.

## Norway examples
- **Hordaland**: a diversified, interactive system where collective work legitimizes a green shift. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 15)
- **Rogaland**: a specialized, dirigiste system where individual work delegitimizes it. (Fløysand et al. 2022, p. 15)
- **Mongstad microalgae**: a pilot that would join oil knowledge to marine knowledge in fish feed. (Fløysand & Jakobsen 2016, p. 337)

- **Intro:** I read the question as asking how a regional innovation system can help or hinder industrial restructuring, and I answer with the concept, related variety and two Norwegian regions, Hordaland and Rogaland.
- **Theory:** Cooke's definition treats a regional innovation system as interacting subsystems of knowledge and exploitation. Fløysand and Jakobsen (2016) add related variety: industries that differ but are related give a region room to combine them, and so to renew without starting from nothing.
