# Paper 7: Stam & van de Ven (2021)
<!-- card: P07 | cite: Stam & van de Ven 2021 | pages: 809-832 | class: 4 | themes: TH4 -->
*Entrepreneurial ecosystem elements*, Small Business Economics 56.

## In one line
The authors define the ecosystem, give it ten elements, and turn them into an index for the twelve Dutch provinces.

## Key concepts
- **Entrepreneurial ecosystem** (*entreprenørielt økosystem*): citing Hawley, a system marked by co-evolution, mutualistic interdependence and diverse, nested actors; it begins when entrepreneurs' paths cross. (p. 811)
- **Productive entrepreneurship** (*produktivt entreprenørskap*): citing Baumol, the firms that commercialise innovations and grow, the model's output. (p. 813)
- **Partisan mutual adjustment** (*partisk gjensidig tilpasning*): citing Lindblom, coordination without a coordinator. (p. 812)
- **Upward and downward causation** (*oppadgående og nedadgående kausalitet*): the elements explain activity, and activity feeds back into the elements. (p. 814)
- **Tautology** (*tautologi*): inferring a good ecosystem from the success it is meant to explain. (p. 810)

## Model
**Ten elements in three groups** (p. 813):
1. Institutional arrangements: formal institutions, culture and networks.
2. Resources: infrastructure, finance, leadership, talent, knowledge, intermediate services and demand.
3. Output: productive entrepreneurship.

## Findings
- The index explains between four-tenths and six-tenths of the variation in high-growth firms. (p. 827)
- The authors call the tests indications of tendencies, not causal proof. (p. 827)
- Utrecht scores 12.42 on the index and Drenthe 5.99. (p. 826)
