# Paper 16: Miörner & Trippl (2017)
<!-- card: P16 | cite: Miörner & Trippl 2017 | pages: 481-497 | class: 5 | themes: TH5 -->
*Paving the way for new regional industrial paths: actors and modes of change in Scania's games industry*, European Planning Studies 25(3).

## In one line
A constraining region can become enabling when a few actors layer, adapt and reapply what already exists.

## Key concepts
- **Regional environment** (*regionalt miljø*): pre-existing industry plus the organisational and institutional support built over time. (p. 481)
- **Layering** (*lagdeling*): adding a new element to the support structure, such as a policy or an organisation. (p. 486)
- **Adaptation** (*tilpasning*): changing or reorienting existing institutions and organisations. (p. 486)
- **Novel application** (*ny anvendelse*): using existing structures in new, unforeseen ways. (p. 487)
- **Interpretative, network and institutional power** (*fortolkende, nettverks- og institusjonell makt*): citing Sotarauta and Mustikkamäki, the power an actor holds. (p. 486)

## Model
**Three modes of change** (p. 486):
1. Layering: adding something new to the structure.
2. Adaptation: changing what exists.
3. Novel application: reusing what exists in a new way.

## Findings
- A few key individuals combined modes across scales to make the environment enabling. (p. 481)
- The new elements were the Nordic Game Conference, the Game Assembly and Game City. (p. 490)
- Power decides the mode: strong actors layer, weaker ones adapt or reapply. (p. 494)
