*Crowdfunding and innovation: a bibliometric review and future research agenda*, European Journal of Innovation Management 27(4).

## In one line
A bibliometric review maps how crowdfunding research meets innovation research, and sets an agenda for what is still open.

## Key concepts
- **Crowdfunding** (*folkefinansiering*): collective financing that also works as an instrument to foster innovation. (p. 1110)
- **Incremental innovativeness** (*inkrementell innovativitet*): citing Chan and Parhankangas, a cumulative improvement of existing technology and capabilities. (p. 1115)
- **Radical innovativeness** (*radikal innovativitet*): citing Chan and Parhankangas, change that is more drastic, unique and comprehensive. (p. 1115)
- **Openness** (*åpenhet*): citing Stanko and Henard, seeking ideas from outside, which crowdfunding embodies. (p. 1116)
- **Social capital** (*sosial kapital*): citing Nahapiet and Ghoshal, resources available through a network of relationships. (p. 1117)

## Model
**The bibliometric review** (p. 1113):
1. Search Scopus and screen with PRISMA.
2. Map co-citations and keyword co-occurrence.
3. Place themes on a centrality and density map.

## Findings
- Two thousand five hundred and forty-four records were reduced to 150 studies. (p. 1112)
- Co-citation gives three clusters: innovativeness, openness and platform innovation. (p. 1121)
- On the thematic map reward-based crowdfunding is a motor theme, while finance stays emerging. (p. 1120)

## Limits
- Scopus was the only source, so work indexed elsewhere may be missing. (p. 1127)
- Articles in languages other than English were excluded. (p. 1127)

## Hook
One hundred and fifty studies, three clusters, one agenda.
